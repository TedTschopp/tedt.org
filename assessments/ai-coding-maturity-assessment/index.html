Keep the Evidence Sources Separate

Each assessment item identifies who or what must demonstrate the behavior. When an item names more than one evidence source, check every named source and the handoff between them.

Team Practice
A recurring human decision, review, approval, meeting, collaboration practice or accountability. Look for observed behavior and records of that behavior.
Practitioner Skill
An ability a person in a named role demonstrates on realistic work. A course-completion record or job title is not a work sample.
AI Workflow and Technology
Behavior enforced or recorded by an AI agent, platform, repository, delivery pipeline, identity service, evaluation service or telemetry system. Look for configuration, run logs and automated outcomes.
For Consequential Decisions: Ask at least two knowledgeable assessors to rate the same scope independently. Keep the eligibility rules and evidence window the same, then reconcile differences against the evidence. Use the assessment for team operating-model planning. Do not use it for individual employment decisions.
