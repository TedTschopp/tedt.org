How the painting works
  1. Build blurred color references for progressively smaller brushes. Brush radius is longest side ÷ pass divisor.
  2. Measure color error: √(ΔR² + ΔG² + ΔB²). Place new strokes where the painting differs from the reference.
  3. Find edges with Sobel gradients of L = 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B. Guide strokes along the perpendicular direction (−Gy, Gx).
  4. Render bristles, washes, dots, matte gouache, broken pastel pigment, or angular knife marks. Finish with a linen or paper texture.

About Quick Study Painter. All image processing runs in your browser. Repaint varies the brushwork. Download includes the selected texture.

Painting engine adapted from Simon Willison's Quick Study under the Apache 2.0 license. Ted's Tools adds gouache, pastel, and palette knife rendering, accessible comparison, and reduced-motion playback.
