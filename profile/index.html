Where a page republishes or adapts another person's work, the original author and source are identified separately from my editorial role. ## Editorial standards I use the following standards for material published on TedT.org: 1. **Identify the kind of work.** Original essays, reprints, quotations, translations, prompts, and reference material should not be presented as though they have the same authorship or provenance. 2. **Name sources and roles.** Reprints identify the source author and original source when that information is available. My role is labeled separately as author, editor, curator, or translation editor. 3. **Preserve chronology.** Historical source dates are kept distinct from the date a page was published or updated on this website. 4. **Link evidence where practical.** Claims based on external reporting, research, or original folklore sources should link to those sources. A link does not imply endorsement. 5. **Distinguish fact, interpretation, and inference.** Commentary and forecasts are presented as analysis, not as inside information or established fact. 6. **Correct material errors.** I update errors, broken attribution, and misleading provenance when I find them. Substantive revisions should receive an updated website date rather than silently changing the historical source date. 7. **Respect ownership.** Reprints and excerpts retain the source author's credit. If you are a rights holder and believe material is incorrectly attributed or used, contact me at [ted@tschopp.org](mailto:ted@tschopp.org). ## AI-assistance disclosure I use AI tools as part of some research, drafting, editing, coding, data-organization, translation, and image-making workflows.
