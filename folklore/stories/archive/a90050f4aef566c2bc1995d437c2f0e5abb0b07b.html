In the Teelwald, a little daughter, Maria Katharina, could not or would not follow the group quickly enough. Growing impatient, the mother tried to frighten the child and told her, ‘If you do not come quickly, the black man will seize you and carry you away.’ The little girl still did not follow as her mother wished, so the latter shouted into the forest, ‘Come, black man, and fetch the lazy child!’", "When the mother soon looked back to see what the child was doing, she could no longer see her anywhere. Believing the girl had run back to the Voralpe, she quickly brought the other children and livestock home and went back up to fetch her. But the little daughter was nowhere to be found. Lamenting, the inconsolable mother gathered people to search for the child—but in vain. On the second day, a little shoe the girl had worn was found in the forest. Only on the evening of the third day did her godmother, Katharina Heinzmann, find her deep in the forest, wedged between two rocks. The child then related that the black man whom her mother had summoned had come and led her there by the hand. He had not spoken a word to her, but neither had he harmed her. She had heard and seen the people both yesterday and the day before, but the black man had kept watch over her and allowed her neither to leave nor to cry out.
