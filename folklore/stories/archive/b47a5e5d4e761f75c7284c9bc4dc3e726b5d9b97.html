One evening he could not fall asleep at all. Then ‘something’ came at 10 o'clock, sat down in the middle of the hay door, and stared steadily at him. Beeseler became terribly thirsty, and the thirst continually increased. He would gladly have left the Gaden to drink some water. ‘Ah, you will go!’ he said to himself. ‘No, you mustn't go!’ he thought again. ‘You don't know what might happen to you.’ All at once he gathered all his courage, rose from his bed of hay, and went out through the hay door. And behold, it kept to one side and let him pass. He was able to quench his thirst undisturbed, and on his return it made room for him again.
