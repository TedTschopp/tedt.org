On one side, the region is lovely and sublime; on the other, desolate and dreadful. Fragrant little flowers, inhabitants of the Alps, sway above the steep slopes, and the solitary ring ouzel sings its melodious song in the dark bushes. The rocks, by contrast, present an uncanny sight, and one need not be surprised that folk belief has placed a ghost there. The one said to have been banished there by a man from Entlebuch named Krummenacher, and which still dwells there, is widely known by the name “Bannhölzler.” It appears here and there in the largest grotto, called the Bannhölzlers Tor, and visits its white horse in another opening in the rock.
