But his comrade persuaded him to endure a little longer, and together they devised a plan to put an end to this torment. On a particular night, the journeyman remained awake in his bed until his tormentor came again; then he immediately threw the halter around the visitor's own neck, and that one was successfully transformed into a horse. Full of joy, he led the horse down to the smithy, where the other journeyman was already waiting. The horse was now shod with iron, afterward mounted by both journeymen, and ridden almost to death. The next morning, the master's wife lay seriously ill in bed but would not reveal what ailed her.
