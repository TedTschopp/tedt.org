Almost every evening the mother went out to amuse herself at lively Abendsitzen with neighbors far into the night. She never took the child along, but put her to bed and locked her in the house. The abandoned little girl often complained to her mother: how could she always go away, stay out so long, and leave her utterly alone? The house was so uncanny, and she was always so terribly afraid! That did no good; the Abendsitzen only lasted longer. Soon the child began to plead in a tearful voice, “Mother, stay with me and do not leave me alone! An evil man comes into the house and wants to carry me away!” The mother did not listen. The following evening, when the poor child noticed that the mother meant to go out again, she began to weep bitterly:", "“Mother,” she lamented, “if you truly will not stay and mean to leave me so alone, at least give me holy water and bless me so that God and my guardian angel may protect me!” But the unloving mother laughed loudly and, as she left, slammed the door behind her.
