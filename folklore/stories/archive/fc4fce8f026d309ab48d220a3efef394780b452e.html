The name Bogenmattpfründe in Oberägeri may also come from this. The legend says that the sisters there made an annual pilgrimage to Einsiedeln and, on those occasions, had the special privilege of walking dry-footed back and forth across the lake without a boat. Once, on the way home at Saint Jost, one of the sisters, being tired, took a Hagstecken with her.
