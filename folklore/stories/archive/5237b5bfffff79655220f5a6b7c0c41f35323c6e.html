The servant dismounted, grasped the child, and tried to lift it, but could not. The two noblemen marveled at this and ordered the other servant to dismount and help. But even together, both men were not strong enough to move it from the spot. After they had tried for a long time, lifting and pulling it back and forth, the child began to speak and said, “Let me lie here, for you cannot lift me from the earth.
