He quickened his steps so that he might offer his services if she had lost her way. As he approached, he noticed that she wore no head covering and walked barefoot. Rain dripped from her magnificent hair, which fell in abundant curls upon her shoulders. A gold chain hung around her lily-white neck; a precious belt encircled her slender waist, and golden bands likewise adorned her arms. Rings set with diamonds glittered on the fingers of her small, snow-white hands. Her bare feet, reddened by cold and wet, seemed so delicate that every little stone must have wounded them. With one hand she modestly held up her silk apron to ease her passage through the rough country, while in the other she carried a long walking staff. She placed her tender feet upon the hard, cold, wet stones so cautiously that one could see each step cost her effort and caused her pain. Her lovely face bore the traces of much weeping; fresh tears still shimmered in her great, gentle eyes, and her fine lips opened in soft sighs and prayers. Filled with wonder at this strange apparition and moved by deep compassion, the herdsman questioned her. She said: ‘I have just left a great city and a splendid palace. My body still lies warm upon its deathbed in Milan, and my parents weep bitterly over it as their only daughter and wet it with their tears. God has sentenced me to atone in this glacier because during my life I scarcely ever stepped upon the earth: I always traveled in a carriage, never went out beneath dripping eaves, never left the house without a stately escort, never exposed myself to a cold breeze, refused myself no respectable pleasure, and feared every exertion and hardship. As punishment for my pampered life, I am therefore condemned to wander barefoot through rain, cold, and storm in this rough wilderness and to atone within this glacier.’ At these last words, a dark, dense fog and cold shower suddenly swept in and hid the lovely figure from his sight. When the storm passed after a few moments and the countryside brightened somewhat, not a trace of the beautiful woman could be seen." ], "notes": [] } } ---
