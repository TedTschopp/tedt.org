Once there was a girl who would not spin, no matter how much she was urged, and talked back to her mother. Then the mother said: ‘Child, take care. The day after tomorrow is Fraufaste, and on Saturday night the Sträggele comes. If you do not spin your assigned stint for me every day, the Sträggele will take you away.’ But it was in vain. When Saturday night came, the Sträggele appeared outside and demanded the Meitschi, the girl. The mother pushed the child outside, thinking she would surely return the next morning. The howling girl was carried away and devoured by the Sträggele. The following morning, people searched and found a few remains of her body scattered through the village. The mother and everyone who heard about it lamented loudly.
