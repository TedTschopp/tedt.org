Commonly called Lienimann, he was a tailor’s son, a foolish and simple person who also had great difficulty speaking because he stammered. He entered the Schiauf Vault, or passage, that extends underground at Augst above Basel and advanced much farther within it than had ever been possible for another human being. He knew how to tell of marvelous events and stories. He related—and people were still living who had heard it from his own mouth—that he took a consecrated wax candle, lit it, and entered the cave. First, he had to pass through an iron gate, then from one vault into another, and finally through several exceedingly beautiful and delightful green gardens. In the middle stood a magnificent and well-built castle or princely house. Within it was a very beautiful maiden with a human body down to the navel. She wore a golden crown upon her head, and her hair flowed loose; below the navel, however, she was a hideous serpent. The maiden took him by the hand and led him to an iron chest upon which two black barking dogs lay, so that no one dared approach it. But she quieted and restrained the dogs for him, allowing him to go forward without hindrance. She then removed a bunch of keys worn around her neck, unlocked the chest, and took out silver and other coins. Out of exceptional generosity, the maiden gave him a considerable quantity, which he carried from the cavern and later displayed for others to see. The maiden also told him that she had been born of royal lineage but had been enchanted and cursed into this monster. Nothing could redeem her except being kissed three times by a young man whose chastity remained pure and unbroken; she would then regain her former shape. In return, she would give and deliver to her redeemer the entire treasure hidden in that place. He further related that he had already kissed the maiden twice. Both times, in her great joy at the unexpected prospect of redemption, she made such dreadful gestures that he became afraid and believed she would tear him apart alive. He therefore did not dare kiss her a third time and went away. Later, several people took him to a house of ill repute, where he sinned with a wanton woman.
