They sent her to the spring below the Breiten to fetch water. The path leads past the Burg, the little hill where Wandelburg Castle stood centuries ago.", "There, a wondrously beautiful female figure appeared to the child, leaned toward her, and asked whether her father might not prefer some wine for once. The child began to cry and said: \"My father is poor and cannot buy wine!\" The woman was pleased by this answer. She took the child by the hand and led her to a heavy iron door in the rock. It opened without a sound, and now they found themselves in a vast vault where two long rows of the finest barrels lay; without doubt, it was the castle cellar of Wandelburg. They stopped by a barrel with two beautifully carved roses. The woman touched it with a little golden wand, and wine sparkling like gold flowed out.", "The overjoyed girl gazed innocently at the lovely figure as though she wished to ask: \"Where am I, and who are you?\" Then the woman kissed the child on the forehead and said:", "\"I am the Castle Maiden Friederike of Wandelburg, and I cannot find rest unless an innocent child like you helps me do so.
