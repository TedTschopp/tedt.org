Natsch", "***", "One of the cows is the witch who is carrying on her evil game here. If one cuts off one of the cow’s ears with a knife, the wicked neighbor is ‘marked’ for the rest of her life. I myself still knew a woman of whom it was commonly said that she always went about with her head bandaged because she had only one ear left. In the same municipality there still lives a man who cut an ear from his own cow because he believed he saw two animals in one chain and wanted to ‘mark’ the witch. I am also told of another man still living who claims to have found his child in the same chain as a goat.
