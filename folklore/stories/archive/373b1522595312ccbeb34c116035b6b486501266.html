Quiet grassy pastures, not yet claimed by any private owner, began directly outside the city gate. A local boy had driven his pigs there. In the idleness of a long summer day, he peeled switches and wove little baskets from them; in doing so, he noticed too late that his boar had slipped away from the herd. He immediately set out to search for it in the forest. He was already imagining all the tribulation and misery that would descend upon him at home because of his carelessness when his animal emerged again from the thicket, leaped among the herd with thoroughly contented bounds, and allowed itself to be driven home with the others. Miraculously, the boar began to smoke and steam so that the vapor could be seen quivering up into the hot midday sun. The herdsman approached, and the boar climbed out.
