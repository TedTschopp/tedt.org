The destroyed Sälischloss was restored as early as the Reformation period and given a castle watchman. This man, called Felix Säli, had a thoroughly supernatural inclination to drink water in unusual quantities. He continued his thirst for water from the year 1547, when he was made castle watchman, until the year 1635, when he was still alive and drinking. Thus, for 88 years, he was a water-drinking castle watchman.
