His mother, however, encouraged him, saying that one must not be discourteous to strangers and that, if they had something evil in mind, his patron saint would protect him. The boy said nothing more, obeyed, took his staff, and walked ahead of the strangers. They spoke in the welsch tongue, and the boy pretended not to understand a syllable. In reality he grasped every word, and because he heard strange things, he listened to everything they said.
