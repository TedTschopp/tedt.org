The cellar of one of these houses is said to fill with water at the same time as the Heiterechsee, which lies far away and several hundred feet lower. The water disappears again at both places at the same moment, as soon as the event it foretold has begun. Even the Swiss Sonderbundskrieg of 1847 is said to have been announced here in this manner.
