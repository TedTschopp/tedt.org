When he advanced against the place to punish them, they fled to the Bötzberg in the Jura. Many inhabitants who hated the Roman yoke joined them, including a young man who had to leave his bride behind, sick and bedridden. As a farewell, he wanted to pick the first flowers of the native spring for her. But because the primroses quickly wilted, he drew water from an unnoticed spring and placed them in it. To his amazement, they suddenly became fresh and fragrant again. In grateful reverence, he now cast them as an offering into the spring full of life-giving power and advised his bride to bathe in the same water. She did so and was soon healed.
