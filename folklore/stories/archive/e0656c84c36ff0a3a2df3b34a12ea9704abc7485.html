Behind them, where the sea of ice now lies, was a mighty lake. When its narrow outlet cleft became closed by masses of ice, the lake rose enormously; then it broke through and destroyed the poor people's land. Saint Martinus, a gigantic man, now brought help: he braced himself against the Mettenberg and pushed the Eiger back with a staff. As a result, his body pressed itself into the rock wall; an unsuccessful thrust created the Martinsloch; but at last the lake nevertheless drained through the newly widened cleft.
