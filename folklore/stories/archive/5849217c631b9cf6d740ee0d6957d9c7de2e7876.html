The mountain walls now draw closer together, the forest grows steeper, and isolated rock pinnacles thrust freshly cut and sun-yellow from it. Then a sudden roar sweeps around the mountain corner. Here the Ergolz descends from a transverse rock wall on the upper plateau into our quiet valley. In a graceful arc, the stream springs in a single bound from its cluster of trees down onto the meadow, where it has scoured a basin as perfectly circular as if a stonemason had cut it.
