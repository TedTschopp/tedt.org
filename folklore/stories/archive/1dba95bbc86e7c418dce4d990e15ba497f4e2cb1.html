Then it sometimes happened that the powerful waters suddenly broke through and plunged into the valley in a devastating wave, where people and livestock perished miserably. The people, who were always threatened, called upon Heaven for help. Heaven sent them Saint Martin, a man of tremendous physical strength. Saint Martin therefore climbed into the mountains, examined the problem, and then braced himself against the Mettenberg. With his staff he pushed the Eiger backward. One failed thrust struck a hole in the rock wall through which the sun still shines once each year on Saint Martin’s Day. The saint’s body, however, pressed so strongly into the rock wall that its imprint can still be seen today on the Bäregg path.
