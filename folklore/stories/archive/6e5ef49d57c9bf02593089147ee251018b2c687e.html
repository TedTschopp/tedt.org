The servant would have liked beyond measure to attend or listen to such a gathering, and so he earnestly begged his master to smuggle him inside just once. Day and night, he pestered and wearied the man about it. At last, the master gave in, rolled the curious servant inside a roll of linoleum, and had him left in a corner of the room. The Freemasons appeared and began their proceedings. After some time, however, a voice called out that displeased the concealed man and did not seem to come from one of the Freemasons: someone was inside who did not belong there; they should see to it that he came out.
