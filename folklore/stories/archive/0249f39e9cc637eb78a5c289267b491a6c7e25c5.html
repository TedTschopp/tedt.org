He had already reached the very edge of a terrible precipice when, at the last instant, he managed to cling with his right hand to a dry piece of dog dung and save himself from the fatal fall. One evening in Linthal in the canton of Glarus, he entered a mountain cave. Curious, he trudged onward and penetrated ever deeper. At last, when he heard the morning prayer bell ringing in Linthal, he saw daylight again and emerged through the Spinnenloch in the Axenfluh on Lake Uri.
