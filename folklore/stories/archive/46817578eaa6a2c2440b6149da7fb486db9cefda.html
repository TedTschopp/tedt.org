He put the bundle of flax into his sack and walked through the mossy grass. Then he heard a voice call to him, ‘Hans, where are you going?’ He looked around but saw no one. The voice called a second time, ‘Hans, where are you going?’ Because he saw no one, he thought, ‘Go on shouting,’ and continued. But when it called a third time, he went back several steps and saw a toad sitting among the stalks, asking where he was going. He said, ‘Mother gave me this bundle of flax, and now I am looking for a spinner, but I doubt I shall find one!’ The toad cried, ‘Give me the flax!’ He said, ‘Well, what will you do with it?’ The toad replied, ‘Give it here.
