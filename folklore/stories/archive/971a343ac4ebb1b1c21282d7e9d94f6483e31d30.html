She therefore promised the arme Seelen a holy Mass if they would drive him home just once. One evening, as the man was on his way home and had reached the Lychghirmi at Tosenden Stein, he was about to take off his hat before the cross there when suddenly something rustled in the hazel bushes beside the road and drove him forward as swiftly as an arrow on the wind; he could not say how. Along the way, he resolved to show due reverence to the cross by the Lychghirmi in Schluchen and raised his hand. But scarcely had he taken his hat in his hand when he was already ten minutes’ walking distance beyond the spot. He arrived home bathed in sweat; a drop of sweat hung from every hair. The entire distance from Tosenden Stein to his house, normally about 3/4 of an hour, he had covered that evening in three minutes. But it did the trick.
