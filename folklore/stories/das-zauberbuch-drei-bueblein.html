One day, on the Ruossdiele—or the cuckoo knows where—they got hold of an old book of magic. With its help they made themselves invisible and conjured themselves into the parish priest's cattle barn, where in all modesty they enjoyed the foam from the freshly drawn milk: “And there they skimmed the foam.” At that time the parish priest Peter Anton Furrer (d. 1883) served in Seelisberg, and he owned an estate and a considerable herd of cattle. Yet while the three rascals feasted on the milk foam, they were also at home, likewise invisible, talking or reading half aloud from their precious book. One evening their mother asked: “Where are you that I can hear you talking and yet cannot see you anywhere?” “We are in the priest's barn, skimming the foam,” the boys confessed. “Yes, but how do you do that? I can hear you here in the room,” their mother inquired further, and received this answer: “We are reading from a book.” “Then put it down!” she ordered. They laid it on the table and became visible again.", "The Christian woman packed up the uncanny book and brought it to the priest, who burned it.
