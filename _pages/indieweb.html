Respond from your website

Publish a response on your own site and include a link to the article you are responding to. Use the article’s Webmention form to send your response. Trusted sources are accepted automatically; other sources are reviewed. Approved responses appear after the next refresh and site update.

Read responses to this website or browse recent writing.

Read and save

The site owner can sign in to Monocle with tedt.org to read feeds in the Aperture account. Aperture’s free service retains seven days of feed history. Bookmarks published to tedt.org are saved permanently.

Publish from an app

{% if site.indieweb.micropub_endpoint %}

The site owner can sign in to Quill with tedt.org to publish articles, notes, photos, replies, likes, reposts, bookmarks, RSVPs, audio, video, events, and check-ins with a compatible Micropub client.

Save a draft to keep it private. Public entries appear after validation and a successful site update. Photos, audio, and video become available before the entry that references them. Client uploads can be up to 5 MiB.
