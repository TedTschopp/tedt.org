**TheOneRing.com Legacy Archive**

This is a personal, noncommercial historical archive preserving selected articles, essays, news items, and community writing that originally appeared on TheOneRing.com during the earlier fan-site era.

This archive exists to preserve a piece of Tolkien fandom history that is no longer generally available online. It is not a relaunch of the original site, not a competing publication, and not operated by, endorsed by, or affiliated with the current operators of TheOneRing.com.

Original bylines, publication dates, and source context are preserved wherever known. Copyright remains with the original authors or rights holders unless otherwise noted. Corrections, attribution updates, permissions questions, or removal requests welcomed.
