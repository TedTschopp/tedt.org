At loveholidays, Non-Engineers Ship to Production

Travel platform loveholidays used OpenAI's Codex to enable product managers and non-engineers to turn customer-experience ideas into working software. Ten experiences were created; three reached the live site.

Why it matters: When prototyping moves closer to domain experts, the product development cycle compresses and engineering specialists focus on hardening promising ideas rather than building speculative ones. Three in ten reaching production is a meaningful early signal for a pattern likely to spread rapidly across industries with high-volume customer-facing surfaces.

What should I do as a leader? Identify one or two teams with strong domain expertise but limited engineering bandwidth. Run a 90-day Codex pilot and measure prototype-to-production conversion and time-to-live.

Cohere Launches Parse for Enterprise Document Intelligence

Cohere's Parse is designed to extract structured information from complex PDFs, charts, tables, and embedded content with high fidelity—targeting the unstructured data problem that blocks enterprise RAG and AI search initiatives.

Why it matters: Document parsing is foundational infrastructure for enterprise AI. If your data lives in contracts, reports, invoices, or slide decks, you cannot build reliable AI workflows until that data can be extracted accurately. Parse enters a market with established competitors, and Cohere's comparisons may not capture the full landscape—including lower-cost alternatives—but any team planning a RAG deployment should add it to their evaluation.

What should I do as a leader? Request a Parse pilot alongside your current shortlist. Cost-per-page and accuracy on your specific document types are the benchmarks that matter most.

Claude Cowork Now Carries Memory Across Sessions

Anthropic has connected persistent memory across Claude's chat interface and its Cowork environment, so users no longer need to manually re-establish context when moving between conversational and agentic work modes.

Why it matters: Persistent memory is quietly becoming a differentiator in enterprise AI tooling. For enterprise rollouts, this also raises governance questions: who owns persisted memory, what is retained, and how is it protected?

What should I do as a leader? Before expanding Claude Cowork deployments, review Anthropic's data-retention and memory-governance policies with your security and legal teams.
