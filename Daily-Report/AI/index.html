The CIO's New Job: AI FinOps at Scale

McKinsey is direct: AI programs routinely blow past their budgets, and most organizations still lack the governance frameworks to manage it. The prescription is AI FinOps — applying financial-operations discipline to model consumption, unpredictable usage pricing, and the growing number of teams spinning up AI workloads independently.

Why it matters: The combination of consumption-based pricing, expanding model options, and agentic workflows that run autonomously at scale makes AI spending a genuine governance risk. Without unit economics, demand governance, and per-team accountability, finance and IT will collide when the bills arrive. This is the operational layer most enterprises skipped in the rush to deploy.

What should I do as a leader? Stand up AI cost-visibility dashboards now, assign clear ownership, and establish usage thresholds per team — before agentic workloads scale further and create unbudgeted exposure.

What should I do? Understand how your AI tool usage is measured and charged back. Invisible consumption becomes a budget surprise and, in some organizations, a compliance finding.

Netflix Shows What Serious Production LLM Infrastructure Looks Like

Netflix published a detailed technical account of its internal LLM-serving stack, including its use of constrained decoding — a technique that enforces output requirements directly during token generation, not as a post-processing filter. The result is compliance baked into the model's output path rather than layered on top.

Why it matters: For regulated industries, this is compliance-by-design: outputs are structurally constrained before they ever leave the model. The architecture also makes the full operational cost of in-house AI honest: custom infrastructure, reliability engineering, and ongoing cost management at scale. For most enterprises, this benchmark clarifies the real tradeoff between full ownership and managed APIs — and raises the question of which outputs in your organization genuinely require this level of control.

What should I do as a leader? Assess your AI governance stack against this standard. If your AI outputs carry regulatory, legal, or safety obligations, investigate constrained-decoding or structured-output capabilities from your model provider — and decide where you need to own the stack.

Why it matters: Physical AI — robotics, industrial automation, autonomous vehicles — is the next major frontier for enterprise AI investment.
