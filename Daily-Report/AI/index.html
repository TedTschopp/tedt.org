The Verifiability Thesis: Where AI Advances Most Reliably

Analyst Jon Stokes argues that AI advances most reliably in domains where outputs can be objectively verified — code, math, logic — while prose and creativity remain dependent on human judgment. The emerging implication: provenance and audit trails will matter more than raw quality scores.

Why it matters: For compliance teams, this is a practical heuristic — lead with verifiable domains and build human checkpoints into subjective ones. Expect disclosure requirements around AI-generated output in regulated industries.

What should I do as a leader?

  • Segment AI use cases into "verifiable output" and "judgment-dependent" buckets; apply proportionally stronger audit controls to the latter.
