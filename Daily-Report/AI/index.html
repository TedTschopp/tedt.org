Elena Verna's sharply worded piece argues that the AI industry has developed a performance-art problem: companies loudly announce AI adoption while quietly struggling to connect any of it to business value. Why it matters: This names something AI practitioners inside large enterprises already feel — the gap between AI announcements and realized value is widening. As executive pressure to demonstrate AI progress intensifies, the risk of measuring the wrong things grows proportionally. Metrics like "employees trained" or "models deployed" are not outcomes; they are activities.

What should I do as a leader? Audit your current AI success metrics. Can each one be traced to a business outcome your organization was trying to improve before AI entered the picture? If not, recalibrate before your next investor or board presentation.
