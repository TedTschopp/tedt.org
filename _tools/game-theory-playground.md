Start with [The Trust Machine](/game-theory/trust-machine/), or explore the
[Game Theory Playground](/game-theory/) to choose an experiment.

Each experiment begins with a prediction, gives you a consequential choice,
and shows the incentives behind the result. Change a setting to restart. Use
“Share these settings” to copy a scenario link; links do not include play history.
