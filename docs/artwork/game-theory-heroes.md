## Shared Art Direction

Every image was generated with the following prompt followed by its subject prompt below.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: an editorial hero photograph for TedT.org's Game Theory Playground, a friendly educational site for junior-high readers and adults. Create a landscape 16:9 image, ideally 1536 by 864 pixels. Style: photorealistic studio photograph of a beautifully crafted physical tabletop teaching model, tactile painted wood and matte ceramic on a warm off-white workbench, subtle visible material grain, soft side lighting and believable soft shadows. Colors: deep navy blue #00446F, warm ivory #F8F6F0, with small burnt-orange #E86027 and cyan #00A9E0 accents. Three-quarter overhead camera, cohesive restrained editorial still life, adult craftsmanship with the approachable clarity of a science museum exhibit. Keep all meaningful objects inside the central 80% of the frame with breathing room around them. Use tangible objects that clearly suggest the concept; this is conceptual hero artwork, not a labeled explanatory diagram. No text, letters, numbers, mathematical symbols, arrows, logos, watermarks, or people. No gradient backdrop, glossy plastic, neon, floating objects, or impossible connections.
